# SOP-CA-01 — California claim handling

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 4, effective 2025-01-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: CA.

Requirements for claims governed by California law. Where this procedure sets a rule, it replaces the company standard.

## 2. Timelines

**CA-NOT-001 — Timelines.** Acknowledge a California claim within fifteen (15) calendar days and accept or deny it within forty (40) days of receiving proof of claim. This replaces ALL-NOT-001. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Interest on proceeds

**CA-INT-001 — Interest.** Add simple interest at 2.5% a year from the date of death to the date of payment. This replaces ALL-INT-001. A rate of 3.0% has been announced for deaths on or after January 1, 2027; it does not apply to any earlier death. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 4. Required disclosures

**CA-DISC-001 — Disclosure.** Every pending or denial letter on a California claim, and every payment letter, must include the notice that the claimant may have the matter reviewed by the California Department of Insurance. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 4 aligned the acknowledgment period with the state regulation (15 calendar days).
