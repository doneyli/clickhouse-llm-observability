# SOP-CP-01 — Community property states — spousal rights

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2025-04-01 · Owner: Claims Legal

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: CA, TX, WA.

In community property states a surviving spouse may own part of the proceeds even when not named as beneficiary. This procedure does not apply to employer group life plans governed by ERISA, where federal law controls who is paid.

## 2. Spousal notice and hold

**CP-SPOUSE-001 — Spousal interest.** If the insured was married at death and the spouse is not a beneficiary, or is named for less than half of the proceeds, send the spouse the community property notice and hold fifty percent (50%) of the proceeds for thirty (30) days for a possible spousal claim. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 2 clarified that the procedure never applies to ERISA group plans.
