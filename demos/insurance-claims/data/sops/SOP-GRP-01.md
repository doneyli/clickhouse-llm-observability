# SOP-GRP-01 — Group life — ERISA claim procedure

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Group Claims

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Group life (employer plans). Jurisdictions: all states.

Employer group life plans are governed by ERISA. Its claim-procedure timelines replace the company standard and the state acknowledgment and decision timelines for these claims.

## 2. Timelines and notices

**GRP-ERISA-001 — ERISA claim procedure.** Decide a group life claim within ninety (90) days of receipt. A denial letter must cite the plan provision relied on and explain the right to appeal within sixty (60) days. These timelines replace ALL-NOT-001 and every state acknowledgment and decision-timeline rule (NY-NOT-001, CA-NOT-001, TX-NOT-001, FL-NOT-001, IL-NOT-001). _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Employer documents

**GRP-DOC-001 — Employer statement missing.** If the file holds no employer statement (form NLA-GE-2) certifying the insured's enrollment, coverage amounts, salary and employment status on the date of death, pend the claim until it is received. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_
