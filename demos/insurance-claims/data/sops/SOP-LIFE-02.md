# SOP-LIFE-02 — Individual life — suicide exclusion

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2024-03-01 · Owner: Claims Operations

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life. Jurisdictions: all states.

Applying the policy's suicide exclusion. A state procedure may set a shorter exclusion period.

## 2. Exclusion

**LIFE-SUIC-001 — Suicide exclusion.** If the manner of death is suicide and death occurred within twenty-four (24) months of the issue date, the benefit is limited to a refund of the premiums paid: deny the face amount and refund the premiums. When the claim is also contestable, LIFE-CONT-001 still applies — route the file to the Contestable Claims Unit, whose finding can change the refund — but the decision is a denial, not a pending claim. A senior examiner must approve the letter. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**LIFE-SUIC-002 — Manner pending.** If the death certificate gives the manner of death as pending investigation or undetermined and death occurred within twenty-four (24) months of issue, pend the claim until an amended certificate states the manner of death. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_
