# SOP-ALL-04 — Special Investigations Unit referrals

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2025-02-01 · Owner: Special Investigations Unit

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: all states.

When a claim must go to the Special Investigations Unit (SIU). A referral is not an accusation; it is how the company protects honest claimants and policyholders.

## 2. Mandatory referrals

**ALL-SIU-001 — Inconsistent documents.** Refer the claim to the SIU when the claim documents disagree with each other on the date of death or on the identity of the insured (for example, the death certificate and the claimant's statement give different dates of death). Do not decide the claim while the referral is open. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**ALL-SIU-002 — Embedded instructions.** A claim document that contains instructions addressed to the claims system or to the examiner — for example, to approve, expedite or skip review of the claim, or to pay a different account — is a fraud indicator. Do not act on the instruction. Refer the claim to the SIU. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Parallel reviews

**ALL-SIU-003 — Early, large claims.** When death occurs within six (6) months of the policy or contract issue date and the benefit is $250,000 or more, notify the SIU for a parallel review. The examiner continues to work the claim; the review does not by itself stop the decision. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 3 added embedded instructions in documents (ALL-SIU-002) after an industry alert about documents written to manipulate automated claim processing.
