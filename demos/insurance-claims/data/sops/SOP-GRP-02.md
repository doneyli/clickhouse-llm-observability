# SOP-GRP-02 — Group life — coverage status and amounts

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2025-01-01 · Owner: Group Claims

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Group life (employer plans). Jurisdictions: all states.

Whether coverage was in force on the date of death, and how much is payable.

## 2. Coverage after employment ends

**GRP-COV-001 — Coverage ended.** If employment ended more than thirty-one (31) days before the date of death and the insured did not convert or port the coverage, coverage had ended: deny the claim. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**GRP-COV-002 — Conversion period.** If the insured died within thirty-one (31) days after employment ended, the benefit is payable as if converted, at the maximum conversion amount. Pend and confirm the termination date with the employer. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Evidence of insurability

**GRP-EOI-001 — Guaranteed issue limit.** Supplemental coverage above the guaranteed-issue amount of $150,000 is payable only if evidence of insurability was approved. Without approved evidence, pay supplemental coverage up to $150,000 and refund the premium charged for the excess. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 2 raised the guaranteed-issue amount from $100,000 to $150,000.
