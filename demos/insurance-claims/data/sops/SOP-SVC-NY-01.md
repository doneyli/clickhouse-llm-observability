# SOP-SVC-NY-01 — New York in-force annuity transactions

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2025-01-01 · Owner: Annuity Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Deferred annuities — in-force servicing (withdrawals, surrenders, replacements). Jurisdictions: NY.

New York applies the best-interest standard to in-force transactions too. Where this procedure sets a rule, it replaces the company standard.

## 2. Best interest

**NY-SVC-001 — Best-interest basis.** For a recommended in-force transaction on a New York contract, give the consumer the written basis for the recommendation — why it is in their best interest given their profile. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**NY-SVC-002 — Free look.** In New York the free-look period on a replacement is sixty (60) days. This replaces SVC-REP-005. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 2 extended the written best-interest basis to in-force transactions.
