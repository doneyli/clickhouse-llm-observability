# SOP-SVC-01 — Annuity withdrawals and surrenders

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2025-03-01 · Owner: Annuity Operations

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Deferred annuities — in-force servicing (withdrawals, surrenders, replacements). Jurisdictions: all states.

Partial withdrawals and full surrenders requested by the owner of a deferred annuity.

## 2. Surrender charges

**SVC-WD-001 — Surrender charge.** Each contract year the owner may withdraw up to ten percent (10%) of the contract value free of charge. On any amount above the free withdrawal amount, apply the surrender charge for the contract year from the schedule — 7%, 6%, 5%, 4%, 3%, 2% and 1% in contract years one to seven, none from year eight — and show it to the owner before processing. A replacement surrenders the existing contract, so the charge applies to it too. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-WD-002 — Early distribution.** If the owner is younger than fifty-nine and a half, the confirmation must disclose that the taxable part of the distribution may be subject to an additional 10% federal tax. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-WD-003 — Withholding election missing.** If the file holds no withholding election (form W-4R) for the distribution, hold the request and ask the owner for it. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-WD-004 — Rider forfeiture.** A full surrender (not a replacement — see SVC-REP-004) ends any guaranteed income or death-benefit rider. If the contract carries one and the owner has not signed the rider-forfeiture acknowledgment, pend the surrender until it is signed. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Required minimum distributions

**SVC-RMD-001 — RMD.** For a qualified contract whose owner is seventy-three or older, check the year's required minimum distribution. If part of it is still due, process the remaining RMD first and tell the owner how much of this request satisfies it. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 3 (March 2025) aligned the RMD age with federal law (73). Version 2 used age 72.
