# SOP-ALL-01 — Death claim intake and required documents

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 4, effective 2025-07-01 · Owner: Claims Operations

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: all states.

This procedure sets out the documents every death claim file must contain before an examiner may recommend payment, and what the examiner does when a document is missing.

## 2. Proof of death

Proof of death is the foundation of the file. Photocopies, funeral-home notices and obituaries are useful context but are never proof.

**ALL-DOC-001 — Certified death certificate.** A certified copy of the death certificate, issued by the vital-records office of the place of death, must be in the file. If the file holds no death certificate, or only an uncertified copy, the claim is placed in pending status and the claimant is asked for a certified copy. No benefit may be paid until it arrives.

## 3. Claimant forms

Each person claiming a share of the proceeds completes their own forms.

**ALL-DOC-002 — Claimant's statement.** Every claimant must return a signed Claimant's Statement (form NLA-CS-1). Where it is missing the examiner pends the claim and sends the form; the claim cannot be decided on a telephone notice alone.

**ALL-DOC-003 — Taxpayer certification.** A signed IRS Form W-9 is required from each payee before funds are released. A missing W-9 holds the payment, not the decision; the examiner requests it with the decision letter.

## Revision history

Version 4 (July 2025) moved the W-9 requirement here from the payments manual. Version 3 accepted a funeral-home statement of death for claims under $10,000; that exception no longer exists.
