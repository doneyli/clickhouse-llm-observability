# SOP-ANN-01 — Annuity death benefits

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2025-06-01 · Owner: Annuity Operations

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: all states.

Death claims on deferred annuity contracts.

## 2. Benefit amount

**ANN-DB-001 — Standard death benefit.** The death benefit is the greater of the contract value on the date the claim is approved and the premiums paid less any withdrawals. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Beneficiary options

**ANN-SPOUSE-001 — Spousal continuation.** When the surviving spouse is the sole primary beneficiary, offer spousal continuation of the contract in writing before paying any lump sum. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**ANN-DIST-001 — Distribution options.** For a qualified contract whose beneficiary is not the spouse as sole beneficiary, send the distribution options letter (lump sum or distribution over ten years) within thirty (30) days. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**ANN-TAX-001 — Withholding election missing.** If the file holds no withholding election (form W-4R) from the payee, hold the claim and request it; taxable gain cannot be paid without it. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 4. Commission

**ANN-COMM-001 — Chargeback.** If the annuitant dies within six (6) months of the contract issue date and the issue commission was paid, notify Agency Operations to charge back one hundred percent (100%) of it. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 3 moved the 10-year distribution option into ANN-DIST-001.
