# SOP-ALL-07 — Interest on death proceeds (company standard)

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Finance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: all states.

Interest the company adds to death proceeds. A state procedure for the governing state replaces this standard where it sets its own interest rule.

## 1. Standard interest

**ALL-INT-001 — Standard interest.** Add simple interest at 2.0% a year on the benefit from the date of death to the date of payment. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

No material changes since version 2.
