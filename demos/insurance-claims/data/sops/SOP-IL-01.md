# SOP-IL-01 — Illinois claim handling

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: IL.

Requirements for claims governed by Illinois law. (IL here is the state of Illinois.)

## 2. Timelines

**IL-NOT-001 — Timelines.** Acknowledge an Illinois claim within fifteen (15) working days. This replaces ALL-NOT-001.

## 3. Interest on proceeds

**IL-INT-001 — Interest.** Add interest at 9% a year on the benefit from the thirty-first (31st) day after the company received due proof of death to the date of payment. No interest is due for the first thirty days. This replaces ALL-INT-001.

## 4. Required disclosures

**IL-DISC-001 — Disclosure.** Denial letters and any letter that delays payment must include the Illinois Department of Insurance notice.

No material changes since version 2.
