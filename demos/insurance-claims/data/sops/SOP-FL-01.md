# SOP-FL-01 — Florida claim handling

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: FL.

Requirements for claims governed by Florida law.

## 2. Timelines

**FL-NOT-001 — Timelines.** Acknowledge a Florida claim within fourteen (14) calendar days and pay within twenty (20) days of receiving due proof. This replaces ALL-NOT-001. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Interest on proceeds

**FL-INT-001 — Interest.** Add simple interest at 3.0% a year from the date of death to the date of payment. This replaces ALL-INT-001. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 4. Required disclosures

**FL-DISC-001 — Disclosure.** Every claim letter — acknowledgment, pending, denial or payment — must include the Florida Division of Consumer Services contact notice. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

No material changes since version 2.
