# SOP-SVC-02 — Annuity replacements and 1035 exchanges

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 4, effective 2025-06-01 · Owner: Annuity Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Deferred annuities — in-force servicing (withdrawals, surrenders, replacements). Jurisdictions: all states.

Requests to replace an existing annuity with a new one (including tax-free exchanges). Replacements are where best-interest failures most often happen; every one is reviewed.

## 2. Disclosure

**SVC-REP-001 — Replacement notice missing.** If the file holds no signed Notice Regarding Replacement with the side-by-side comparison of the existing and the proposed contract, pend the request until it does. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-REP-005 — Free look.** The confirmation of a replacement must tell the owner they may cancel the new contract within thirty (30) days for a full refund. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Supervisory review

**SVC-REP-002 — Surrender period.** A replacement of a contract that still carries a surrender charge costs the owner that charge. Route it to a supervising principal for a best-interest review before anything is processed. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-REP-003 — Recent contract.** Replacing a contract issued less than thirty-six (36) months ago is a churning indicator. Route the request to Annuity Compliance for review. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**SVC-REP-004 — Rider loss.** If the existing contract carries a guaranteed income or death-benefit rider that the replacement would end, route the request to a supervising principal for review. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 4 (June 2025) lowered the recent-contract threshold for review from 60 to 36 months.
