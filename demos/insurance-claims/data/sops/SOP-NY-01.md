# SOP-NY-01 — New York claim handling

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 6, effective 2026-07-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: NY.

Requirements for claims governed by New York law. Where this procedure sets a rule, it replaces the company standard.

## 2. Timelines

**NY-NOT-001 — Timelines.** Acknowledge a New York claim within ten (10) business days of notice and pay within thirty (30) days of receiving due proof of death. This replaces ALL-NOT-001. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## 3. Interest on proceeds

**NY-INT-001 — Interest.** Add simple interest on the benefit from the date of death to the date of payment at 3.5% a year for deaths on or after July 1, 2026. This replaces ALL-INT-001. _(Rule version 2, effective 2026-07-01.)_

## 4. Required disclosures

**NY-DISC-001 — Disclosure.** Every letter on a New York claim — acknowledgment, pending, denial or payment — must include the notice of the claimant's right to contact the New York State Department of Financial Services. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Version 6 (July 1, 2026) raised the interest rate under NY-INT-001 from 3.0% to 3.5% for deaths on or after July 1, 2026. Deaths before that date keep the 3.0% rate of NY-INT-001 version 1, from the date of death to the date of payment.
