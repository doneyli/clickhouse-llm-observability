# SOP-NJ-01 — New Jersey claim handling

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: NJ.

Requirements for claims governed by New Jersey law. New Jersey claims otherwise follow the company standards for timelines and interest.

## 2. Required disclosures

**NJ-DISC-001 — Disclosure.** Every claim letter must include the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance contact notice. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

No material changes since version 2.
