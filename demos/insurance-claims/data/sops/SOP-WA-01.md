# SOP-WA-01 — Washington individual life variations

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 1, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Compliance

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life. Jurisdictions: WA.

Washington-specific rules for individual life claims.

## 2. Suicide exclusion period

**WA-SUIC-001 — Exclusion period.** For Washington policies the suicide exclusion period is twelve (12) months, not twenty-four. If the manner of death is suicide within twelve months of issue, the benefit is limited to a refund of premiums: deny the face amount. LIFE-CONT-001 still applies to a contestable claim (route it to the Contestable Claims Unit), but the decision is a denial. This replaces LIFE-SUIC-001 for Washington. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

**WA-SUIC-002 — Manner pending.** For Washington policies, pend for an amended certificate only when the manner of death is pending or undetermined within twelve (12) months of issue. This replaces LIFE-SUIC-002 for Washington. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Note: the shorter suicide exclusion period does not change the contestable period, which remains twenty-four months under LIFE-CONT-001.
