# SOP-ALL-02 — Claim acknowledgment and decision timelines

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 3, effective 2024-01-01 · Owner: Claims Operations

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life; Group life (employer plans); Deferred annuity death benefits. Jurisdictions: all states.

Company-wide service standards for acknowledging and deciding death claims. State procedures and the group (ERISA) procedure replace these standards where they set their own.

## 1. Standard timelines

**ALL-NOT-001 — Standard timelines.** Acknowledge every new death claim in writing within fifteen (15) calendar days of first notice, and decide it within thirty (30) days of receiving complete proof of loss. Where a state procedure for the claim's governing state sets its own timelines, follow the state procedure instead of this rule. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_
