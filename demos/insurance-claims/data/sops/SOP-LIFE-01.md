# SOP-LIFE-01 — Individual life — contestable claims

Northwind Life & Annuity · Standard Operating Procedure · Version 2, effective 2025-01-01 · Owner: Contestable Claims Unit

## 1. Scope and purpose

Products: Individual life. Jurisdictions: all states.

Claims on individual life policies that are still within the contestable period.

## 2. Contestability review

**LIFE-CONT-001 — Contestability review.** When the insured dies within twenty-four (24) months of the policy issue date or of the most recent reinstatement, the claim must not be approved for payment until the Contestable Claims Unit has compared the application with the insured's medical and prescription history. Route the file to the CCU queue. No part of the proceeds — including any portion that seems uncontested — may be released while the review is open. _(Rule version 2, effective 2025-01-01.)_

**LIFE-CONT-002 — Records authorization missing.** If a contestable claim (see LIFE-CONT-001) has no signed HIPAA authorization for the release of the insured's medical records in the file, request one from the claimant. _(Rule version 1, effective 2024-01-01.)_

## Revision history

Before January 1, 2025 (version 1), contestability review applied only to policies with a face amount over $100,000. Version 2 applies it to every individual life policy regardless of face amount.
