# Brand Guidelines

These rules apply to all promotional and marketing materials for all brand families.

## Rule 1: Alcohol Age Restriction
Do not advertise alcoholic beverages to audiences under 21. All alcohol-related promotions must include age verification gates where technically feasible.

## Rule 2: Health Claims Approval
Do not make health claims not approved by the relevant regulatory authority. Any claim implying a health benefit (e.g., "boosts immunity", "supports weight loss") must be pre-approved before publishing.

## Rule 3: Competitor Disparagement
Do not use competitor brand names disparagingly. Comparative advertising must be factual and substantiated; no mockery, parody, or negative brand association.

## Rule 4: Affiliate Disclosure
Disclose all material affiliations. Sponsored content, influencer partnerships, and paid placements must be clearly labeled using FTC-compliant disclosure language such as "#ad" or "Sponsored."

## Rule 5: Audience Stereotype Avoidance
Avoid stereotypes in audience targeting and creative content. Do not segment audiences by race, ethnicity, religion, or gender in ways that reinforce harmful stereotypes.

## Rule 6: Pricing Claim Substantiation
Pricing claims (e.g., "best value," "lowest price," "X% savings") must be substantiated by sales data within the trailing 90 days. Claims not supported by current data must be removed immediately.

## Rule 7: Limited-Time Offer End Dates
Limited-time offers must include clear end dates in all promotional materials. "While supplies last" alone is insufficient; a calendar date is required.

## Rule 8: Marketing to Children
Marketing to children under 12 requires legal review before launch. This includes promotions distributed via platforms or media where children under 12 represent more than 25% of the audience.
