# Regulatory Rules Reference

This document covers rules from the regulatory bodies governing our operations.
All promotional campaigns must be reviewed against applicable regulations before launch.

---

## Federal Food Authority

### Food Safety
- All food products must carry accurate ingredient lists and allergen declarations.
- Nutritional claims (e.g., "low sodium", "reduced fat") must meet the authority's quantitative thresholds.
- Products marketed as "natural" must not contain artificial preservatives, colors, or flavors.

### Labeling Standards
- Net weight or volume must be declared on all packaging per current standards.
- Country of origin labeling is required for primary ingredients exceeding 5% of composition.
- Expiration or "best by" dates must be present and formatted per current standards.

---

## Federal Trade Authority

### Advertising Fairness
- All advertising claims must be truthful, not misleading, and substantiated by evidence at the time of publication.
- Endorsements and testimonials must reflect the honest opinions of the endorser. Material connections must be disclosed.
- Deceptive pricing is prohibited. "Was / Now" pricing claims require a bona-fide prior offering period of at least 30 days.

### Pricing Fairness
- "Sale" pricing must represent a genuine reduction from the regular price.
- Free offer promotions must disclose all conditions, including purchase requirements, in plain language.
- Sweepstakes and contests must not require a purchase to enter and must clearly state odds and prize values.

### Marketing-to-Minors
- Digital advertising targeted to children under 13 must comply with applicable child privacy laws (COPPA).
- No behavioral targeting may be applied to audiences identified as under 13.
- Promotional materials directed at children under 12 must not pressure children to purchase or persuade parents.

---

## State Beverage Boards

### Alcohol Marketing
- Alcohol advertising must not target audiences where individuals under 21 represent more than 28.4% of the audience (DISCUS standard).
- Promotions offering discounts on alcohol must comply with individual state minimum pricing laws where applicable.
- Sampling events for alcoholic beverages require advance permits from the relevant state board.
- Health and wellness claims about alcoholic beverages are prohibited unless specifically authorized.

### Distribution Compliance
- Three-tier distribution laws in applicable states prohibit direct manufacturer-to-retailer arrangements unless an exemption applies.
- Self-distribution is only permissible in states that have enacted specific craft beverage exemptions.
- Import and inter-state shipment of alcohol requires applicable federal and state permits.

---

## General Compliance Notes
- When a campaign spans multiple regulatory jurisdictions, the most restrictive rule applies unless a jurisdiction-specific exemption is documented and approved.
- Campaigns must be reviewed by Legal at least 5 business days before launch when any of the following apply: alcohol products, health claims, promotions targeting audiences with a known proportion of minors, or sweepstakes/contests.
