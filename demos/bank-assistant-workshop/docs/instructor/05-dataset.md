**Frame:** "A dataset states what good behavior means for this product."

**Show:** Review the eight local examples against the fictional KB. Explain input, reference and metadata. Mark reviewed references, then seed the dedicated dataset.

**Learner work:** Have pairs review one English and one Spanish policy item. Draft a safe synthetic reproduction of a monitored failure.

**Land:** "Reviewed expected behavior is the foundation of an interpretable comparison."

**Ask:** "Who in your organization owns the correct reference answer?"

**Common pitfalls:** Draft references are not ground truth. The --reviewed flag and per-item review markers are required. Stable IDs upsert; removed local items do not delete remote items.

**Recovery:** Use the reviewed seed set in the instructor project for a demonstration. Do not skip review and call it a golden dataset.
